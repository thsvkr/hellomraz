Horrifier returns with its second album - primitive accelerated proto-death with great respect for Autopsy, tightly mixed with classic horror slasher films of the 70s-80s. Songs about the REJOICE, CHILDREN OF THE FLESH, HUMAN BUTCHER and MORBID LUST, everything is as it should be. Norway has never been the birthplace of death metal since the country's black wave, but it seems the youth are determined to change the situation. Forward, youth!
