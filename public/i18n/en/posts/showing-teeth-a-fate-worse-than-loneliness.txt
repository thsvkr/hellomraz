The debut EP of an interesting internet phenomenon - it’s not every day that you see a metalcore band signed to normal labels even before the release of the debut EP. What adds interest here is that the project is in fact assembled from disparate professional and not-so-professional musicians, some of whom had previously pursued their influencer careers on social networks (the vocalist and driving force of the Addison band had previously achieved popularity on TikTok by recording her vocal covers while driving her Honda Accord). Impressive female vocals (especially clean), excellent production and good own material for the first EP - your children's (if you have children, subscriber) new unashamed favorite metal bands have arrived.
