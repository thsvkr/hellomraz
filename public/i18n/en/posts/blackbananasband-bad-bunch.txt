The first album in 12 years by Black Bananas, a band led by Jennifer Herrema, one half of the legendary Royal Trux. Hairy metal and rock were thrown back, the group came closer to pop music and this made it even stranger - we have a strange home-made and very hypnotic fusion of dub, funk, psychedelic rock, thigh electronics and old school hip-hop. It is difficult to understand what is happening, but it is even more difficult to tear yourself away from listening.
